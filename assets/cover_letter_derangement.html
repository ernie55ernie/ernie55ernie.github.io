5 Firms, 5 Cover Letters

Each personalized letter is randomly placed into an envelope. What is the probability that no letter goes to the correct firm?

A permutation with no fixed points is called a derangement.

Why are there 44 derangements?

Use inclusion–exclusion. Start with all 120 assignments, subtract those where at least one letter is correct, then add back cases counted twice, and so on.
