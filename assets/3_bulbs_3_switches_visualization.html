The one-trip solution

  1. Turn Switch 1 ON and leave it on for several minutes.
  2. Turn Switch 1 OFF.
  3. Turn Switch 2 ON. Leave Switch 3 OFF.
  4. Enter the room once.
Now inspect the bulbs:
💡 Lit bulb → Switch 2
♨️ Dark but warm bulb → Switch 1
❄️ Dark and cold bulb → Switch 3
Key idea: residual heat stores information about the past. That gives each bulb three useful observable states: lit, dark-but-warm, and dark-and-cold.
