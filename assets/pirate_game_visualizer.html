Why this works

Pirates' priorities

  1. Survive.
  2. Get more gold.
  3. If otherwise tied, prefer the proposer to die.

The key inequality

A pirate who survives after rejection with x coins must receive:

x + 1

to vote “yes”. If that pirate would die after rejection, survival itself can secure the vote for 0 coins.

Standard Pirate Game assumptions: proposer votes, ties pass, pirates rank survival first, gold second, and killing the proposer third.
