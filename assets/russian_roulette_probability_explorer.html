Variant 2: why 5/36 is not Player 2's final risk

5/36 is only the chance that Player 2 is shot on the first cycle. With probability (5/6)^2 = 25/36, both players survive and the game restarts in the same state.

Player 1 eventual risk
1/6 + (5/6)^2(1/6) + ... = 6/11 ≈ 54.55%
Player 2 eventual risk
(5/6)(1/6) + (5/6)^3(1/6) + ... = 5/11 ≈ 45.45%
