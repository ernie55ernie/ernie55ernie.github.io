Mr. & Mrs. Jones Handshake Puzzle

Nine people report different handshake counts: 0,1,2,...,8. People never shake hands with themselves or their spouse.

Interactive elimination

Repeatedly pair the smallest and largest remaining counts.

Start with the extreme answers: 0 and 8.

Why 0 must pair with 8

The person who says 8 shook hands with everyone they were allowed to shake hands with: all 8 non-spouses.

But the person who says 0 shook nobody. Therefore the 8-person could not have shaken the 0-person, so those two must be spouses.
