Rope-Burning Puzzle

You have two ropes. Each takes exactly 60 minutes to burn from one end, but neither burns uniformly along its length. Use them to measure exactly 45 minutes.

What to do
Light both ends of Rope A and one end of Rope B.

Rope A is being consumed at twice the total burn rate, so it finishes after 30 minutes.

Key insight

1
0 → 30: Rope A burns from both ends. Rope B burns from one.
2
At 30: Rope A is gone. This is a reliable 30-minute signal.
3
30 → 45: Light Rope B’s second end. Its remaining 30 burn-minutes are consumed in 15 minutes.
Measured time
30 + 15 = 45

The ropes need not burn uniformly along their physical length. The only assumption is that each rope takes exactly 60 minutes to burn completely from one end.
