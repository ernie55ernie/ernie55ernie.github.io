1. Define the state

Let Pᵢ be the probability of reaching N before 0, starting from i.

2. Look one step ahead

Pᵢ = pPᵢ₊₁ + qPᵢ₋₁

With probability p you move to i+1; with probability q you move to i−1.

3. Boundary conditions

P₀ = 0     Pₙ = 1

Closed-form solution

How to derive the formula

① Rearrange the recurrence pPᵢ₊₁ − Pᵢ + qPᵢ₋₁ = 0
② Solve the difference equation The characteristic roots are 1 and q/p.
③ Use the two boundaries Enforce P₀=0 and Pₙ=1 to determine the constants.
Final answer

If p = q = 1/2:
Pᵢ = i / N
Otherwise:
Pᵢ = [1 − (q/p)ⁱ] / [1 − (q/p)ᴺ]
