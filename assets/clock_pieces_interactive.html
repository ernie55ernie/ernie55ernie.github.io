Clock Pieces

Break a clock face numbered 1–12 into 3 connected pieces so that the sum of the numbers on every piece is the same.

THE CHALLENGE
Break the clock into 3 pieces

Every number from 1 to 12 must stay on exactly one ordinary-looking piece, and all three pieces must have the same sum.

The two straight cracks isolate a top cap, a connected middle strip, and a bottom cap. Every piece has the same total: 26.

Key observation: 1 + 2 + ··· + 12 = 78, so each of the three pieces must sum to 26. Pairing opposite-value complements gives 13: (1,12), (2,11), …, (6,7).
