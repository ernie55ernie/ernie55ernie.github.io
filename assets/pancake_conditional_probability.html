Pancake Conditional Probability

You randomly choose one of three pancakes — GG, BB, or GB — then observe that the visible side is golden. What is the probability that the hidden side is also golden?

Of the 3 possible golden faces you could have observed, 2 belong to the GG pancake. Therefore, the probability that the hidden side is also golden is:

P(other side is G | visible side is G) = 2/3

Why not 1/2?
After seeing gold, the remaining pancakes are not equally likely. GG is twice as likely as GB because GG has two golden faces that could have produced your observation.
