11 Pirates and the Majority Safe

Design a lock-and-key system so that any 6 or more pirates can open the safe, but no group of 5 or fewer can.

The construction

Make one lock for every possible set of 5 pirates. For a lock labeled by a 5-person set S, give copies of that key to exactly the 6 pirates not in S.

Why it works

≤5
Any forbidden group of at most 5 pirates is contained in some 5-person set S. None of them has the key to lock L(S), so they cannot open the safe.
≥6
For every 5-person set S, a group of 6 or more must contain at least one pirate outside S. That pirate has the key to L(S), so every lock can be opened.

Core idea: focus on the maximal forbidden groups, which are the 5-person subsets. Give each such group its own lock that none of those 5 pirates can open. Therefore the number of locks is C(11,5) = 462.
