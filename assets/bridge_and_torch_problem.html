Bridge and Torch Problem

Four people take 1, 2, 5, and 8 minutes to cross a narrow bridge. At most two people can cross at once, they must carry the torch, and a pair moves at the slower person's speed. Can everyone cross within 15 minutes?

Goal: move all four people to the far side.

The key idea

A tempting strategy is to have the 1-minute person escort everyone individually. That takes 17 minutes. The trick is to send the two slowest people together, so the 8-minute cost is paid only once.
