Why it works

1. Broadcast parity

The first caller announces whether the number of red hats among the other 99 is even or odd.

2. One missing bit

A later prisoner sees every hat in that 99-person set except their own.

3. Recover the missing bit

Comparing the visible parity with the announced parity reveals whether their own hat is red or blue.

Why 100 guaranteed survivors is impossible: the first caller sees the same 99 hats whether their own hat is red or blue. Any fixed guess must be wrong in one of those two worlds.
