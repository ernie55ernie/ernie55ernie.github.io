Families Keep Having Children Until They Have a Girl

Each birth is independent, with probability 1/2 of being a girl.

CORE IDEA
50% girls

Every family ends with exactly one girl. Before that girl, there may be 0, 1, 2, 3, ... boys.

E[boys] = 0(1/2)+1(1/4)+2(1/8)+... = 1
Girl fraction = 1 / (1+1) = 1/2
